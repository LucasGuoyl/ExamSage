# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ExamSage a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or course of study.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give and accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for students and the community, respect privacy and intellectual property, and acknowledge uncertainty honestly.

Harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing, publication of private educational material, credential exposure, and deliberate academic-integrity abuse are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate these standards, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's available private contact channel.

This document is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
